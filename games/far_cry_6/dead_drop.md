## 1. Interrogation in the basement

### Description

Dani is captured.

### Observation

Antón Castillo and General Raúl Sánchez hold Dani Rojas captive. During the interrogation Dani's molars are pulled. Raúl tries to cut Dani's throat; Antón's son Diego shoots Raúl in the head. Dani escapes from the burning basement.

### Interpretation

The torture is carried out; only the killing that follows is stopped.
