Jason has joined Hoyt Volker's privateers under the name Foster to find his brother Riley.

## 1. Torturing Riley

### Description

Interrogation in the basement while Hoyt watches the room.

### Observation

Hoyt tells Jason to interrogate and torture a prisoner in the basement. The prisoner is Jason's brother Riley. Jason hurts Riley, among other things by pressing his thumb into Riley's bullet wound.

### Interpretation

Jason tortures his own brother to keep his cover in front of Hoyt; the act is real, not staged.
