## 1. Losing a finger

### Description

The poker game.

### Observation

Hoyt says he will cut off one finger for each hand Jason loses. After the second lost hand, Hoyt cuts off Jason's left ring finger with a knife. A knife fight with quick time events follows.

### Interpretation

One finger is cut off, not several. The game turns the threat into a game of chance with a fixed outcome.
