# Whitetail Mountains

Game: [Far Cry 5](game.md).

## 0. Whitetail Mountains

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Kind | region and its story missions The World Is Weak, We Must Be Strong, Sacrifice the Weak, Only You |
| Characters | the Junior Deputy; Jacob Seed; Staci Pratt; Eli Palmer |

Jacob Seed runs the region for Eden's Gate and abducts the Junior Deputy several times.

## 1. Captivity and conditioning

### Description

Captivity scenes with Jacob Seed.

### Observation

Jacob Seed holds the Junior Deputy and Staci Pratt captive. According to the wiki he starves his subjects for days. He conditions them with a music box that plays the song "Only You". In Sacrifice the Weak the Deputy has been unconscious for seven days and is fed after waking.

### Interpretation

Psychological torture through deprivation and conditioning.

## 2. Triggered rampages

### Description

Playable sections after Jacob opens the music box.

### Observation

Jacob opens the music box, and the Deputy goes on a rampage that the player plays. In The World Is Weak two Whitetail Militia prisoners die. In Only You the Deputy shoots Eli, the leader of the Whitetail Militia.

### Interpretation

The player carries out the effect of the conditioning; the killing of an ally is played, not watched.
