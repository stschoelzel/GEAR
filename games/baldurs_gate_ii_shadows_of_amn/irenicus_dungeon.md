# Irenicus's Dungeon

Game: [Baldur's Gate II: Shadows of Amn](game.md).

## 0. Irenicus's Dungeon

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | Chapter 1, start of the game |
| Location | Jon Irenicus's dungeon, area AR0602 (level 1) and level 2 |
| Characters | Jon Irenicus; companions Imoen, Jaheira, Minsc, Khalid |

The game starts in a cell in Jon Irenicus's dungeon. The player character and the companions are his prisoners and have to find a way out.

## 1. Experiments in the cell

### Description

Opening of the game in the cell, before the player has control.

### Observation

The game opens in a cell. Irenicus performs physical and magical experiments on the player character. Imoen says that Irenicus tortured her as well, and opens the player character's cell. Jaheira and Minsc are held in cages next to it.

### Interpretation

The player starts as a victim of torture, together with the whole party. The game introduces its antagonist through what he has done to the player character, not through what the player sees him do.

## 2. Khalid's body

### Description

Second level of the dungeon.

### Observation

On the second level the party finds the body of the companion Khalid on a table.

### Interpretation

The torture of Khalid is shown through its result, not through the act.
