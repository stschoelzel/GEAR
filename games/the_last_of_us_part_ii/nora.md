## 1. Beating Nora

### Description

### Observation

Ellie demands to know where Abby is. A button prompt (Square on PlayStation) appears. Each press is a blow by Ellie. The scene goes on only when the player presses it.

### Interpretation

The game makes the blows depend on the player's input; the player cannot go on without carrying them out.
