## 1. Breaking a suspect

### Description

Interrogation of a suspect.

### Observation

According to Sample (2008), the player interrogates suspects on the spot in a minigame to get information that is needed at once.

### Interpretation

The game turns interrogation under time pressure into a skill test, following the ticking bomb scenario of the series.
