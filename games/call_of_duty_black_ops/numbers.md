# Numbers

Game: [Call of Duty: Black Ops](game.md).

## 0. Numbers

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Mission | 7 of the campaign |
| Location | Kowloon City, Hong Kong; in-game date 9 February 1968, 1700 hours |
| Playable | Jason Hudson; in the frame story Alex Mason |
| Other characters | Grigori Weaver; Dr. Daniel Clarke, chemical engineer; an interrogator |

Hudson and Weaver look for Clarke to learn about the nerve agent Nova 6 and the numbers broadcast. The campaign is framed by Mason's interrogation, which returns between the missions.

## 1. Mason in the interrogation chair

### Description

Frame story before the mission.

### Observation

An interrogator asks Alex Mason about the meaning and the origin of the numbers broadcast.

### Interpretation

The player character of the frame story is the one interrogated.

Open: The transcript shows a verbal interrogation. Whether the frame story shows physical torture, such as electric shocks, is not covered by it.

## 2. Hudson questions Clarke

### Description

Interrogation of Clarke, with Weaver present.

### Observation

Hudson breaks a window pane and puts pieces of glass into Clarke's mouth. Hudson punches Clarke until his lips bleed. Clarke gives information about Nova 6 and the numbers.

### Interpretation

Within one mission the player is the interrogator, while the frame story makes the other player character the interrogated one.
