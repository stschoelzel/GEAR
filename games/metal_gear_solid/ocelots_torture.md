## 1. Enduring the electric shocks

### Description

Snake is strapped to Ocelot's machine.

### Observation

Snake is strapped to Ocelot's electric shock machine; the wiki gives it a rating of up to 4,000 volts and 500 psi. During each shock the player presses the Circle button (B on other platforms) again and again.

### Interpretation

The player resists the torture through input; the victim role is played, not watched.

Open: What happens if the player gives up, and whether this changes the ending, is not yet documented.
