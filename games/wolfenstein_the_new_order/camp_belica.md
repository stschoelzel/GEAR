# Camp Belica

Game: [Wolfenstein: The New Order](game.md).

## 0. Camp Belica

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Chapter | 8 |
| Location | Camp Belica, a fictional concentration camp; control room; crematorium |
| Characters | B.J. Blazkowicz; an officer called The Knife |

Blazkowicz goes into the camp disguised as a prisoner.

## 1. In the chair

### Description

Blazkowicz is caught in the control room.

### Observation

Blazkowicz is strapped to a chair. The officer called The Knife tortures him and stabs him in the torso. During the scene the player can only move the camera.

### Interpretation

The restricted control keeps the player in the victim's body without letting them act.

Open: Whether the scene serves to justify the violence against the guards that follows, as participants of a focus group read it.

## 2. Waking among the dead

### Description

After the torture.

### Observation

Blazkowicz loses consciousness. He wakes with five health points on a pile of bodies in front of the furnace.

### Interpretation

The low health carries the torture over into play.
