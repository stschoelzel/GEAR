## 1. Interrogating a captive

### Description

Interrogation of a captive.

### Observation

Sam Fisher holds a captive. The player moves him through the room and uses force and objects nearby to get information. The scenes run without camera cuts.

### Interpretation

In interviews, creative director Maxime Béland says the interrogations have no camera cuts so that players feel in control throughout ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/splinter-cell-blacklist-wont-have-interactive-torture-5980350), [GamesIndustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/ubisoft-cuts-torture-scene-from-splinter-cell-blacklist)).
