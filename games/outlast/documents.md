### Description

### Interpretation

The documents report the experiments in the language of the institution; the notes report what the player character has seen. Torture appears in both registers.

## 1. Organized Torture

### Description

Notes in chapter 4, Male Ward.

### Observation

Note 12 "Organized Torture" unlocks when the player films writing on the wall of a dark room in night-vision mode. Note 13 "Dr. Rick Trager" unlocks when the player, hidden under a bed, films Trager killing a victim strapped to a bed; this comes after the escape from Trager's wheelchair. Note 14 "Death of Trager" unlocks when the player films Trager's body after he is crushed by the elevator.

### Interpretation

The scene in which Trager tortures the player character is documented in [Trager](trager.md).

Open: The wording of the three notes is not yet recorded.

## 2. MKULTRA Program Excerpt

### Description

A document between the Male Ward and the Courtyard.

### Observation

The document is an excerpt from a CIA file headed "To: File", subject "Special Research, Bluebird", with the reference "MORI doc no. 140401". It asks whether sleep instruction and hypnotic techniques are of value for war and Agency problems. Among its questions: "Can we create by post-H control an action contrary to an individual's basic moral principles?" Further questions concern total amnesia, lasting changes of personality and a "sleep-inducing machine".

### Interpretation

The game places a document from the real history of US research on interrogation and behaviour control among its fictional files. It ties the fictional experiments of Murkoff to MKUltra; see [Underground Lab](underground_lab.md), section 1.

Open: The location differs between sources (Outlast Wiki: next to the doorway to the Courtyard before leaving the Male Ward; GamesRadar: chapter 5, on the floor near the stairs past the fountain). Open: Check the text against the released CIA document.

## 3. Patient status report for Chris Walker

### Description

A document in chapter 1, Administration Block, on a desk in a document storage room.

### Observation

The document is a Murkoff form headed "Project Walrider", "Patient Status Report", case number 136, patient initials "CLW", observing physician Dr. Rudolf Wernicke. It records that "Morphogenic Engine activity plateaued at roughly 2000 ppm" and that it is "Unsafe to progress beyond stage 3 hormone schedule". Walker "was interviewed in restraints, following his self-inflicted mutilations"; the restraints had to be altered for his size. It notes "dermal eruptions" from failed Morphogenic Engine cellular activity.

### Interpretation

The experiment appears as clinical administration: case number, measured values, treatment stages, restraint as a technical detail. See [torture as research](../../tags/torture.md#torture-as-research).
