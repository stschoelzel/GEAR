# Outlast

## Description

First-person survival horror game, developed and published by Red Barrels. Released for Windows on 2013-09-04, PlayStation 4 on 2014-02-04 and Xbox One in June 2014; later also macOS, Linux and Nintendo Switch. The player is the journalist Miles Upshur in Mount Massive Asylum, run by the Murkoff Corporation. The player character has no weapons; the player can run, hide and film with a camcorder.

The downloadable content Outlast: Whistleblower (2014) tells a parallel story with the software engineer Waylon Park as player character. Entries on Whistleblower name it under `first_appeared`.
