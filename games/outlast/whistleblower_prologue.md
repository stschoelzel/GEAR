## 1. Waylon in the engine

### Description

Waylon's whistleblowing is discovered.

### Observation

Waylon maintains the Morphogenic Engine, which Murkoff uses to manipulate lucid dreams of comatose patients. After seeing what the engine does to the patients, he sends an anonymous e-mail to Miles Upshur. Blaire discovers the e-mail, has Waylon detained and exposed to the engine.

### Interpretation

The engineer who maintains the machine becomes its test subject. Being put into the experiment works as punishment for whistleblowing, not only as research. Compare the reports in [Documents](documents.md), section 3.
