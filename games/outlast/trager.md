# Trager

Game: [Outlast](game.md).

## 0. Trager in the Male Ward

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | main game, chapter 4, Male Ward |
| Location | Male Ward of Mount Massive Asylum; Trager's operating room; elevator |
| Characters | Miles Upshur; Dr. Richard (Rick) Trager, a former Murkoff executive and patient who calls himself a doctor |
| Previous | chapter 3, Sewer |
| Next | chapter 5, Courtyard |

### Interpretation

The scene is the first of the "helpless scenes" in the series, in which the player character is tortured and escapes at the last moment ([GameRevolution](https://www.gamerevolution.com/originals/14103-outlast-2s-helpless-scene-is-the-best-in-the-series-spoilers-crucifixion), accessed 2026-11-24). The torturer is himself a product of the institution: a former executive who became a patient.

## 1. Capture

### Description

Miles meets Trager in the Male Ward.

### Observation

Trager tells Miles that he "made the right choice" and beats him. The sources differ on whether Miles loses consciousness; afterwards the image is blurred. Trager straps Miles into a wheelchair and pushes him through the ward. He stops at an exit and asks whether Miles wants to leave. The player has no control during the ride.

## 2. Fingers

### Description

In Trager's operating room.

### Observation

Trager talks about faith and capitalism. He cuts off two of Miles' fingers with a large pair of shears: the ring finger of the left hand and the index finger of the right hand. He announces that he will do the same to Miles' tongue and genitals, and leaves the room.

### Interpretation

The player watches the torture from the first-person view of the victim, without input.

## 3. Escape and Trager's death

### Description

From the operating room to the elevator.

### Observation

While Trager is out of the room, Miles frees himself from the wheelchair and vomits at a sink. Trager pursues him. In the moving elevator Trager breaks in; Miles pushes him out. Trager is caught between the elevator gates and crushed between floors. If the player films the body, note 14 "Death of Trager" unlocks.

### Interpretation

The game turns the victim back into a player only after the torture, through flight; there is no possibility to resist during the torture itself. Compare [Metal Gear Solid, Ocelot's torture](../metal_gear_solid/ocelots_torture.md), where resistance is the input.
