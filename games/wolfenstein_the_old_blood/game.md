# Wolfenstein: The Old Blood

## Description

First-person shooter by MachineGames, published by Bethesda Softworks on 5 May 2015 for Windows, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One. Standalone prequel to Wolfenstein: The New Order. In Germany first sold in a version with changed symbols and texts; since 22 November 2019 the international version is also sold there.
