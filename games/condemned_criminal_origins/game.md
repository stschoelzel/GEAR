# Condemned: Criminal Origins

## Description

First-person horror game by Monolith Productions, published by Sega, for Xbox 360 in 2005 and Windows in 2006. The player is the FBI agent Ethan Thomas, who hunts the serial killer Leland Vanhorn (Serial Killer X).
