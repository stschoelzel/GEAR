## 1. Anderson tortured by Vanhorn

### Description

Fate of Carl Anderson.

### Observation

Leland Vanhorn has taken Carl Anderson to Apple Seed Orchard and tortures him with Anderson's own methods. Anderson kills himself on a fire poker.

### Interpretation

The torturer from an earlier level becomes a victim himself. The main antagonist is Vanhorn, not The Torturer.

## 2. Ethan's finger

### Description

Cutscene after Malcolm Vanhorn knocks Ethan out.

### Observation

Malcolm Vanhorn knocks Ethan Thomas out. Leland Vanhorn ties Ethan up, speaks about how he followed Ethan's cases, and cuts off Ethan's index finger.

### Interpretation

The player character becomes a victim of the killer he hunts.
