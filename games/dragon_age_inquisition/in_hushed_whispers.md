# In Hushed Whispers

Game: [Dragon Age: Inquisition](game.md).

## 0. In Hushed Whispers

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | main quest, only if the player sides with the mages |
| Location | Redcliffe Castle, in an alternative future |
| Characters | the Inquisitor, Dorian, Alexius, Fiona, Leliana |

Alexius sends the Inquisitor and Dorian into an alternative future. They have to find a way back to the present.

## 1. Leliana in the torture cell

### Description

In the dungeons of the castle.

### Observation

Fiona tells the Inquisitor where Leliana is held. The Inquisitor and Dorian find Leliana in a torture cell and free her. Leliana has been tortured and used for experiments on her resistance to the Blight. She helps them return to the present.

### Interpretation

The torture of an advisor is shown through its result in a possible future.

Open: How long Leliana was held, one year or two, is given differently in community sources ([Dragon Age Wiki discussion](https://dragonage.fandom.com/f/p/3311529323659413054)). Check against the dialogue.
