## 1. Accepting the ritual

### Description

Abdirak's offer and the ritual that follows.

### Observation

Abdirak offers to hit the player character three times with a ritual weapon. The player chooses a mace, a knife or an axe. If the player accepts, skill checks follow (Performance, Intimidation, or failed Constitution checks). After the ritual the player character has the permanent condition Loviatar's Love: +2 to attack rolls and saving throws while below 30 % of hit points.

### Interpretation

The player character is the one who suffers, by consent, and gets a game advantage for it.
