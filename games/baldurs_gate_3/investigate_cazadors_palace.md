# Investigate Cazador's Palace

Game: [Baldur's Gate 3](game.md).

## 0. Investigate Cazador's Palace

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | Act 3 |
| Location | Cazador's Palace and dungeon |
| Characters | Astarion, Cazador Szarr |

The quest leads to the vampire lord Cazador Szarr, who made the companion Astarion a vampire spawn. It ends with a choice whether Astarion completes Cazador's ritual, which would sacrifice 7,000 souls.

## 1. Astarion's past

### Description

Background to the quest, given in dialogue, texts and the dungeon.

### Observation

Dialogue, texts and the dungeon say that Cazador held and tortured Astarion and 7,000 other vampire spawn for centuries.

### Interpretation

The torture is the companion's backstory. It is told, not shown.

## 2. Astarion and Godey

### Description

Only if the vampire spawn kidnap Astarion during a long rest in the Lower City and the party fails to stop them.

### Observation

Astarion's fellow spawn try to kidnap him while the party rests. If they bring Astarion to 1 hit point, he is taken to Cazador's palace. There Godey tortures him and tries to compel him magically to obey Cazador. The tadpole in Astarion disrupts the spell. Godey becomes hostile, and the party finds Astarion in the kennels.

### Interpretation

The torture of the past is repeated in the present, as a consequence of a failed fight.
