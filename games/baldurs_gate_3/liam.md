# Liam's interrogation

Game: [Baldur's Gate 3](game.md).

## 0. Liam's interrogation

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | Act 1 |
| Location | Shattered Sanctum, goblin camp, torture chamber |
| Characters | Liam (prisoner), Spike (torturer) |

The goblins hold Liam prisoner and want him to reveal where the Druid Grove is.

## 1. Spike tortures Liam

### Description

The player enters the torture chamber.

### Observation

Spike tortures Liam to learn where the Druid Grove is.

### Interpretation

The torture is under way when the player arrives.

## 2. Taking over, stopping or freeing

### Description

The dialogue that follows.

### Observation

In dialogue the player can take over the torture, stop it, or open Liam's restraints and free him. Community reports name approval from the companion Astarion when the player takes part.

### Interpretation

The player can become the torturer, the one who stops it, or the one who frees the victim; all three are offered in the same dialogue.
