# Disasterpiece

Game: [Cyberpunk 2077](game.md).

## 0. Disasterpiece

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | Act 2, main job |
| Location | Judy Álvarez's van; former Electric Corporation power plant |
| Characters | V, Judy Álvarez, Evelyn Parker, the Scavengers |

V and Judy look for Evelyn Parker, who has disappeared.

## 1. Analysing the braindance

### Description

V watches an illegal braindance recording in edit mode.

### Observation

V buys an illegal braindance recording (XBD). The player examines it in edit mode on the visual, thermal and audio layers. The recording shows Scavengers drag a victim to a chair and kill him. The audio layer has screams and a radio that covers them.

### Interpretation

To go on, the player has to watch the recording closely and more than once, as a forensic task.

## 2. Finding Evelyn

### Description

End of the quest.

### Observation

V enters the Scavengers' studio under the power plant and frees Evelyn Parker. Evelyn does not respond to V. According to the sources she was abused physically and sexually.

### Interpretation

Open: How the game shows the abuse (terminals, rooms, dialogue) is not yet checked.
