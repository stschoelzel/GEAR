# They Won't Go When I Go

Game: [Cyberpunk 2077](game.md).

## 0. They Won't Go When I Go

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | side job, end of the chain Sinnerman, There Is a Light That Never Goes Out, They Won't Go When I Go |
| Location | braindance studio in Japantown, Westbrook |
| Characters | V; Joshua Stephenson, convicted murderer; Rachel Casich, producer |

Joshua has a contract with Rachel to record his own crucifixion as a braindance. V can end the chain earlier by killing Joshua in Sinnerman.

## 1. The crucifixion

### Description

The recording of the crucifixion.

### Observation

V can take the hammer and nails and nail Joshua to the cross, watch another person do it, or leave the studio.

### Interpretation

The player can carry out the act on a victim who wants it.
