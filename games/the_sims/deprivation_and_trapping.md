# Deprivation and trapping

Game: [The Sims](game.md).

## 1. Trapping a Sim

### Description

What players do with the build and live modes.

### Observation

The player can change the surroundings so that a Sim cannot meet its needs or cannot leave: a room without doors, no food, a pool without a ladder, blocked exits near a fireplace or indoor fireworks. Depending on the method, the Sim dies of hunger, drowning, fire or electric shock. With the expansion Livin' Large, a Sim can also die of a disease from a neglected guinea pig.

### Interpretation

The game has no torture mechanic; the practice arises from the needs simulation.

Open: Whether trapping and starving a Sim falls under the tag. Players and research describe these practices as torturing Sims ([figure in Kim & Petrina](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Gaming-strategies-to-torture-and-kill-characters_fig3_237660691)).
