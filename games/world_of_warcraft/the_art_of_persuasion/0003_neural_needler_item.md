# Item text of the Neural Needler

Sequence: [The Art of Persuasion](sequence.md).

## Description

Item tooltip of the Prototype Neural Needler, a replacement for the quest item [Neural Needler](0002_using_the_neural_needler.md), handed out by Initiate Mehrtens if the original is destroyed. Conjured item with two charges.

## Observation

The tooltip reads: "Inflicts incredible pain to target, but does no permanent damage."

## Interpretation

The game names the device's purpose plainly and adds that it leaves no permanent damage. See [white torture](../../../tags/torture.md#white-torture).
