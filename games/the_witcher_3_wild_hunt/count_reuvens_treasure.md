## 1. Handing Triss over

### Description

Geralt and Triss are in the headquarters under cover.

### Observation

Caleb Menge wants to take Triss to an interrogation. If the player lets him, Triss's fingernails are torn out while Geralt keeps his cover. If the player refuses, Geralt fights Menge's Witch Hunters at once.

### Interpretation

The player decides whether an ally is tortured for the sake of the plan.
