# I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream

## Description

Point-and-click adventure by The Dreamers Guild, published by Cyberdreams in 1995 for MS-DOS, based on Harlan Ellison's story. Re-released by Night Dive Studios. The supercomputer AM has killed humanity except five people, whom it has kept alive for 109 years to torture them: Benny, Gorrister, Nimdok, Ellen and Ted. Each is put into a scenario built on their trauma or guilt.

In the German version the scenario of Nimdok was removed; the game could therefore not be completed ([Gespielt](https://gespielt.hypotheses.org/7552)).
