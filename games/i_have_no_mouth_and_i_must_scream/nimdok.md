# Nimdok's scenario

Game: [I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream](game.md).

## 0. Nimdok's scenario

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Location | a recreated concentration camp |
| Characters | Nimdok, a former Nazi doctor; prisoners of the camp; AM |

Missing in the German version.

## 1. Victims of experiments

### Description

Nimdok in the camp.

### Observation

The player meets victims of medical experiments, among them a patient whose eyes have been removed and who is wired to a machine.

### Interpretation

The player character is a former perpetrator who now has to face his victims, inside a torture that AM inflicts on him.

## 2. Acting toward the prisoners

### Description

Choices in the scenario.

### Observation

The player can act with compassion or with cruelty toward the prisoners.

### Interpretation

Open: Which actions are possible and what follows from them.
