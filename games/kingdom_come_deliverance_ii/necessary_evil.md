# Necessary Evil

Game: [Kingdom Come: Deliverance II](game.md).

## 0. Necessary Evil

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Part | main quest, after Back in the Saddle |
| Location | Trosky Castle, torture chamber |
| Characters | Henry; Hans Capon; Otto von Bergow; a captured bandit |

Otto von Bergow tells Henry and Hans to find out what the bandit knows. The outcome affects Henry's standing with Hans and later events in the village of Semine.

## 1. Questioning the bandit

### Description

The interrogation.

### Observation

The player can get all the information without torture by passing Speech, Charisma, Intimidation or Deception checks. If the player chooses to, or after failed checks, the player can hurt the prisoner with slaps, punches, tongs and a hammer. The prisoner can die from the torture.

### Interpretation

The player decides whether to torture, and skill decides whether it is needed.

Open: Check whether torture becomes necessary after failed checks, and what changes with Hans and in Semine.
