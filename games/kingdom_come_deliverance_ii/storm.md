## 1. Hanging in the torture chamber

### Description

Henry and Žižka are prisoners.

### Observation

Henry and Jan Žižka hang in the torture chamber. Istvan Toth and the executioner Ernest question and torture them; Father Godwin has to watch. The player can stay silent or try skill checks in dialogue. After failed checks Henry has wounds, low health and a torture debuff during the escape that follows. Godwin gives the information. Katherine frees the prisoners.

### Interpretation

In the same room as in Necessary Evil, the player character is now the victim. The dialogue choices change how badly Henry is hurt, not whether he is tortured.
