# Ordered to torture

Sequence: [By the Book](sequence.md).

## Description

Opening of the mission in the warehouse in Banning, Los Santos, before the first round of torture.

## Observation

Haines tapes Mr. K to a chair and shows him tools from a cart. Michael and Norton drive to an address in Rockford Hills, which turns out to be a false lead. The game switches to Trevor, and Haines tells him to torture Mr. K to find the right target.

## Interpretation

The state agency does not torture itself; it has the most violent of the three protagonists do it. See [outsourced torture](../../../tags/torture.md#outsourced-torture).
