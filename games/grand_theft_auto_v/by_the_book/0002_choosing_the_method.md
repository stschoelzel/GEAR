# Choosing the torture method

Sequence: [By the Book](sequence.md).

## Description

The player as Trevor chooses an instrument from the cart: waterboarding, a wrench, jumper cables for electrocution or pliers to pull a tooth. A heart monitor shows Mr. K's heart rate. Gold medal objectives include using waterboarding, electrocution and pulling a tooth, and finishing without Mr. K's heart stopping.

## Interpretation

The torture is made into a minigame with a skill condition: the player must hurt without killing. The gold medals reward using every method.
