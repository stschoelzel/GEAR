# Drive to the airport

Sequence: [By the Book](sequence.md).

## Description

End of the mission, after the target has been shot.

## Observation

Haines tells Trevor to take care of Mr. K. Trevor drives him to the airport instead and tells him he has to flee; Mr. K wants to go back to his family. At the airport Mr. K stumbles away and falls down a flight of stairs, and the mission ends. During the drive Trevor talks about torture.

## Interpretation

The mission ends with the torturer speaking about the torture he has carried out. Whether this frames the scene as critique or excuse is the central question of the mission's reception.
