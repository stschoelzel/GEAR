# Grand Theft Auto V

## Description

Action-adventure game by Rockstar North, published by Rockstar Games in 2013 and re-released for later console generations and PC. Three playable protagonists: Michael De Santa, Franklin Clinton and Trevor Philips; the player can switch between them in many missions.

## Reference source

[GTA Wiki](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_V). Rockstar offers no official mission database; the community wiki is the common reference. Mission titles and names follow it.
