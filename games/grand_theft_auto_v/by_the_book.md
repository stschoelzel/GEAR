# By the Book

Game: [Grand Theft Auto V](game.md).

## 0. By the Book

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Mission | [By the Book](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/By_the_Book), main story |
| Given by | FIB agent Steve Haines |
| Location | warehouse in Banning, Los Santos; Rockford Hills; beach house in Chumash; airport |
| Protagonists | Michael De Santa, Trevor Philips |
| Other characters | FIB agent Dave Norton; Ferdinand Kerimov, called Mr. K |
| Previous | Three's Company |

The FIB wants Michael to kill Tahir Javan, whom Haines and Norton say has ties to terrorism. Mr. K is their only source on Javan's whereabouts; Trevor stays with him in the warehouse while Michael and Norton search the city.

## 1. Ordered to torture

### Description

Opening of the mission in the warehouse in Banning, Los Santos, before the first round of torture.

### Observation

Haines tapes Mr. K to a chair and shows him tools from a cart. Michael and Norton drive to an address in Rockford Hills, which turns out to be a false lead. The game switches to Trevor, and Haines tells him to torture Mr. K to find the right target.

### Interpretation

The state agency does not torture itself; it has the most violent of the three protagonists do it. See [outsourced torture](../../tags/torture.md#outsourced-torture).

## 2. Choosing the torture method

### Description

The choice of instrument for the torture, as Trevor.

### Observation

The player as Trevor chooses an instrument from the cart: waterboarding, a wrench, jumper cables for electrocution or pliers to pull a tooth. A heart monitor shows Mr. K's heart rate. The gold medal objectives include using waterboarding, electrocution and pulling a tooth, and finishing without Mr. K's heart stopping.

### Interpretation

The torture is set up as a minigame with a skill condition. The gold medals reward using every method.

## 3. Torture after the information

### Description

After the first round of torture. The true location turns out to be a beach house in Chumash, where Michael has to identify and shoot the target with a sniper rifle.

### Observation

Mr. K gives information after the first round of torture. Haines says he has to make sure the information is good and tells Trevor to torture him again with another instrument before Michael can find the target.

### Interpretation

The torture goes on after the information has been given. See [torture past the information](../../tags/torture.md#torture-past-the-information).

## 4. Drive to the airport

### Description

End of the mission, after the target has been shot.

### Observation

Haines tells Trevor to take care of Mr. K. Trevor drives him to the airport instead and tells him he has to flee; Mr. K says he wants to go back to his family. At the airport Mr. K stumbles away and falls down a flight of stairs, and the mission ends. During the drive Trevor talks about torture. He asks whether they got any information out of Mr. K, who answers: "I would have told you everything." Trevor says: "Torture's for the torturer" and calls it "useless as a means of getting information".

### Interpretation

The mission ends with the torturer speaking about the torture he has carried out. Whether this frames the scene as critique or as excuse is open; the reception has not yet been checked.
