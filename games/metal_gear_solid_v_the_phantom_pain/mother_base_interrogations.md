# Interrogations on Mother Base

Game: [Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain](game.md).

## 0. Interrogations on Mother Base

### Description

| | |
|---|---|
| Location | Mother Base, interrogation room |
| Characters | Venom Snake; Revolver Ocelot; Kazuhira Miller; Quiet; Huey Emmerich; Diamond Dogs soldiers |

Ocelot leads interrogations of prisoners on the player's own base, the base of the Diamond Dogs.

## 1. Quiet's interrogation

### Description

Cutscene after Quiet has joined the Diamond Dogs.

### Observation

Quiet sits with a black bag over her head, among male soldiers. A soldier puts two metal batons to her chest; her body convulses. According to GamingBolt, Miller and Ocelot have her tortured for information.

### Interpretation

Torture happens in the player's own organisation; in the earlier games Snake was the victim.

Open: Girina describes the scene as shown in the E3 2013 trailer; check against the released game whether Venom Snake is present and whether the player can step in.

## 2. Huey's interrogation

### Description

Cutscene after Huey is brought to Mother Base.

### Observation

Miller and Ocelot torture Huey Emmerich for answers. Miller believes that Huey sold out the old Mother Base to Cipher; Huey says he is innocent.
