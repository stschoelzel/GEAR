# Torture

## Definition

Inflicting severe pain or suffering on a person in someone's power, to obtain information or a confession, to punish or to intimidate. Used for events in which torture is shown, ordered, described or carried out by the player.

## Aspects

### Outsourced torture

An institution keeps its own rules against torture while someone outside those rules carries out the torture for it, usually the player or the player character. In World of Warcraft the Kirin Tor's code of conduct forbids "extreme" measures, so the outsider does it; in Grand Theft Auto V the FIB has Trevor do it.

Open: Does the explicit framing shift responsibility onto the player, or does it give the player a ready excuse?

### White torture

Torture by means that leave no visible traces on the body. The Neural Needler in World of Warcraft is described as causing pain without permanent damage; the absence of wounds lets the act be repeated as a game action without the visual codes of violence.

Open: The term comes from human rights discourse. Check whether it fits a fantasy setting or needs a narrower definition here.

### Torture past the information

The torture continues after the victim has given the information it was meant to obtain. In World of Warcraft the player can go on using the needler, and the victim calls it senseless; in Grand Theft Auto V the torture is ordered again after the first answer.

Open: Is this a critique of torture as a means of getting information, or does it turn the torture into an end in itself for the player?
